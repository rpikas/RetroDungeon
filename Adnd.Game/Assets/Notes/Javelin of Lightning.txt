Javelin of Lightning
A javelin of lightning is considered equal to a + 2 magic weapon, although it has 
neither "to hit" nor damage bonuses. It has a range of 9" and whenever it strikes, 
the javelin then becomes ihe head of a W* wide, 3" long stroke of lightning. 
Any creature hit by ihe javelin suffers 1-6 hit points of damage, plus 20 hit points 
of electrical damage. Any other creatures in the path of the back stroke rake either 
20 or 10 hit points of damage. (Draw a straight line between point of impact 3" back 
in the direction of the character hurling it.) From 2-5 will be found The javelin 
is consumed in the lightning discharge

What changed
1.	Combat logic for Javelin of Lightning
•	File: Adnd.Core/Combat/Resolution/CombatResolver.cs
•	Added Javelin of Lightning handling in ranged attack resolution:
•	If equipped quantity is 0, attacker cannot throw it.
•	On a successful hit:
•	Normal javelin damage is applied (1d6 from weapon data).
•	Then a lightning discharge is applied:
•	Impact target: +20 electrical damage.
•	Other monsters in the same group (line-path approximation): save for half, taking 20 or 10 electrical damage.
•	The thrown javelin is consumed (Quantity - 1), and removed from Range slot at 0.
•	Added helpers:
•	IsJavelinOfLightning(Item?)
•	ResolveJavelinOfLightningStrike(...)
2.	Item data aligned with requested rules
•	File: Adnd.Data/Items/RangedWeapons.json
•	Updated Javelin of Lightning (3):
•	Damage / DamageVsLarge: changed from 1d6+2 → 1d6
•	Range: changed to 9/18/27 (9" short-range representation)
•	MagicBonus: set to 2 (counts as +2 for magic-hit requirements)
•	Description updated to reflect discharge + consumption behavior
Notes
•	This now matches your requirement that it is effectively a +2 magical weapon for hit immunity checks, but with no intrinsic to-hit/damage bonus.
•	The “3" backstroke line” is approximated using same-group monsters since the combat model does not track battlefield coordinates.
