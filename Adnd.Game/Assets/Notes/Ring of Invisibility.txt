Ring of Invisibility
What is now implemented
•	Added character-level ring invisibility state:
•	RingInvisibilityActive
•	RingInvisibilityAppliedArmorClassBonus
•	RingInvisibilityInaudibilityActive
•	Added ring helper methods on Character:
•	detect equipped Ring of Invisibility in Ring1/Ring2
•	roll and persist the 10% inaudibility trait on that ring (SpecialAbilities)
•	activate/deactivate ring invisibility
•	break invisibility on hostile action
•	break inaudibility when speaking
•	Added equip/unequip safety in EquipmentManager:
•	if Ring of Invisibility is replaced or unequipped, ring invisibility is cleared properly.
•	Added camp activation flow (UseItemAction):
•	if Ring of Invisibility is equipped, player can toggle it at will.
•	when turned on: applies invisibility and AC effect.
•	if that ring is one of the 10% variant, it reports inaudibility too.
•	Added combat interaction:
•	ring invisibility breaks on attack (ResolvePartyAttack).
•	inaudibility breaks on speaking actions in combat:
•	spell casting
•	combat item invocation
•	Added exploration speaking interaction:
•	inaudibility breaks when casting spells in camp (CastSpellAction).
Notes
•	I kept the “speak breaks silence” behavior for spell speaking paths.
•	I did not force “Use Item in camp” to break inaudibility globally, so ring toggle itself can be used cleanly without auto-breaking first.
•	This follows your requested ring semantics while fitting current game architecture.
