Ring of Mammal Control
This ring enables its wearer to exercise complete control over mammals with 
intelligence of 4 or less (animal or semi-intelligent mammals). Up to 30 hit dice of mammals can be 
controlled, Control extends to such limits as to enable the wearer to have the creatures controlled 
actually kill themselves, but complete concentration is required. (Note: the ring does not affect 
bard-mammal combinations, humans, semi-humans, and monsters such as lammasu, shedu, manes, cores, etc.) 
If you are in doubt about any monster, it is NOT a mammal
Obviously, rats, weasels, herd animals, dolphins, and even unicorns are mammals, but intelligence will 
preclude control of the better ones. Control time is 3 segments.
