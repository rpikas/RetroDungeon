Protection from Magic (Scroll) — Explained Clearly
This scroll creates a 5‑foot radius invisible anti‑magic globe around the reader. Think of it as a mobile anti‑magic shell that follows the character.
Maybe you agree with this interpreation?
Here’s what it actually does:

🔒 1. Blocks ALL magic — both directions
Inside the globe:
No magic can enter
No magic can leave
No magical effects function

This includes:
Spells
Spell‑like abilities
Magic items’ effects
Magical projectiles
Magical barriers
Magic weapon bonuses
Normal physical things still work:
Swords
Arrows
Movement
Talking
Nonmagical equipment

🚶 2. The globe moves with the reader
Just like Protection from Evil, the anti‑magic field is centered on the scroll user and travels with them.

⚠️ 3. Magic items touching the globe must save
This is the dangerous part.
Any magical item that touches the globe must make a saving throw:
50% chance of being drained
Save is 11 or better on d20
Failure = item permanently loses all magic
Success = item is unaffected
This applies to:
Magic weapons
Magic armor
Rings
Wands
Staves
Rods
Potions
Scrolls
ANYTHING magical

🗡️ 4. Multiple items touching at once
If several magic items hit the globe at the same time (example: a fighter with sword + shield + armor):
The leading item is checked first
Example: a sword held forward
Then items are checked in order of decreasing power
As soon as one item fails, the globe ends and that item is drained

This means:
The globe does not drain multiple items
It drains one item, then collapses
The first failed save ends the protection

⏳ 5. Duration
5–30 rounds (5d6)
That’s ½ minute to 3 minutes of real time.

🧙 What this means in play
✔ You become immune to magic
No spell can touch you. No wand can affect you. No magic missile, no charm, no fireball, no magic weapon bonus.
✔ You can walk through magical barriers
Wall of Fire
Wall of Ice

Protection from Evil  
All suppressed.

✔ You can shut down enemy spellcasters
They cannot affect you with magic at all.

❌ Your own magic items become risky
If your +3 sword touches the globe → it must save.
If your +2 armor touches the globe → it must save.
If your ring touches the globe → it must save.

Most characters must drop all magic gear before using this scroll.
